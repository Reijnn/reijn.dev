__Unit__: 20 Infanteriebataljon Bewaken & Beveiligen KNR, Badhoevedorp

__Responsibilities__

* Prepare and teach lessons and practical training for reservists
* Coach students during their training and assess their progress
* Instruct in operating in urban areas (OVG)

__Courses__

ECOD Praktijkleermeester, 2025 · KI OVG Urban Warfare, 2026 · ECOD Mentor, Schietinstructeur Geweer and ECOD Opleider in progress
