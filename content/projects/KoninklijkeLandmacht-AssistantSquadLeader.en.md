As a corporal I was second in command of an infantry squad, and stood in for the squad leader when needed.

__Unit__: 20 Infanteriebataljon Bewaken & Beveiligen KNR, Bergen

__Responsibilities__

* Support the squad leader in preparing and running training and exercises
* Lead the squad when the squad leader is absent
* Instruct and coach soldiers in the squad
* Look after the squad's equipment and readiness

__Courses__

Opleiding Leidinggevend Korporaal (OLK), 2024 · BO-FO Infanterie Bewaken & Beveiligen KNR, 2024 · Basisopleiding Koninklijke Landmacht, 2024 · ECOD Praktijkopleider, 2025
