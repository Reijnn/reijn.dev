As a sergeant I lead an infantry squad in a guarding and security unit of the reserve.

__Unit__: 20 Infanteriebataljon Bewaken & Beveiligen KNR, Bergen

__Responsibilities__

* Lead the squad during guarding and security tasks and exercises
* Plan and run the squad's training
* Coach soldiers and assess their development on the job
* Report to the platoon commander on the squad's readiness
